Ferns and Petals Sales Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data for Ferns and Petals, a leading gifting and floral company. The goal is to derive actionable insights from sales data using Power Query, Power Pivot, Pivot Tables, and Data Visualization in Excel. The dashboard provides key business intelligence metrics related to revenue, customer behavior, product performance, and geographical sales trends.

Features and Insights
Total Revenue: ₹35,20,984
Average Customer Spending: ₹3,520.98
Total Orders Processed: 1,000
Average Delivery Time: 6 Days

Revenue Analysis by:
Occasion (Valentine’s Day, Raksha Bandhan, etc.)
Month and Hour of Order Placement
Product Category and Top-Selling Products
Geographical Distribution of Orders

Tools & Technologies Used
Microsoft Excel: Power Query, Power Pivot, Pivot Tables, Data Visualization
Data Transformation: Power Query for data cleaning and structuring
Data Aggregation: Power Pivot for complex calculations
Visualizations: Charts, Slicers, and Interactive Filters

Implementation Process

Data Collection & Preprocessing:
Extracted and cleaned sales, customer, and product data.
Handled missing values and ensured data consistency.

Data Transformation & Modeling:
Used Power Query to structure and refine data.
Implemented Power Pivot Measures for advanced calculations.

Dashboard Development:
Created interactive visualizations using Pivot Tables & Charts.
Integrated slicers & filters for user-friendly data exploration.
Business Insights & Recommendations:
Identified high-revenue products and peak sales periods.
Suggested data-driven strategies for inventory and marketing improvements.

Business Impact
Optimized marketing strategies by identifying high-sales occasions.
Improved inventory management based on demand trends.
Enhanced customer experience by reducing average delivery times.
Enabled strategic decision-making with data-driven insights.

How to Use the Dashboard
Open the Excel file containing the dashboard.
Use the slicers on the right side to filter data by occasion, month, and gender.
Analyze different charts and pivot tables to explore revenue patterns.
Gain insights into customer behavior, sales trends, and product performance.
